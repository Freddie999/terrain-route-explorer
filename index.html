What the numbers mean

Distance uses a spherical Earth model. Elevation is bilinearly sampled from one zoom-11 Terrarium tile. Grade is the elevation change divided by horizontal distance between samples. Ascent sums positive changes.

Assumptions in plain sight

Routes are illustrative, not verified roads or trails. Terrain pixels are roughly 59 m wide here; denser samples do not add source detail. No buildings, vegetation, airspace, access rights, weather, or obstacle-clearance model is included.
